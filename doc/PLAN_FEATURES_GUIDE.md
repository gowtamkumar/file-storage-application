## 💡 Pro Tip

Keep features **specific and measurable**:
- ✅ "10 GB Storage" - Clear and verifiable
- ✅ "API Access" - Clear feature
- ❌ "Advanced Analytics" - Too vague, not implemented
- ❌ "Premium Support" - Subjective, not a technical feature

This builds **trust** with your customers!
